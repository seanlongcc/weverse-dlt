# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Weverse fans archiving live replays on a Windows desktop. They collect several links at once, want the original video and replay chat, and may leave a queue running while doing other things. The interface also supports Linux when its browser and media dependencies are installed.

## Product Purpose

Turn Weverse replay links into organized video, title, chat JSON, ASS overlay, and optional burned-in MP4 files. Help users connect their own Weverse session without developer-console work, track each replay, and recover from individual failures.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clear, personal. This is a focused desktop utility with a little warmth, not a marketing site.

## Anti-references

Avoid the previous saturated pink panels, oversized decorative headers, walls of equally prominent controls, and hidden progress. Do not make routine success depend on dismissing dialogs.

## Design Principles

- Put replay links and queue actions at the center of the workspace.
- Keep connection setup compact, with manual credentials available as a fallback.
- Show both queue progress and the current replay's output stages.
- Keep every output accessible, including partial results after a failure or cancellation.
- Offer light and dark appearances without changing workflow capabilities.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use native keyboard-operable Qt controls, visible focus, descriptive labels, readable contrast, and text alongside status colors. Keep the workspace usable at smaller desktop window sizes with scrolling. Store appearance preferences only; session cookies stay out of settings and logs.
